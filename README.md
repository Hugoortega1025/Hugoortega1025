## Featured Projects

### 1. Watson Healthcare Employee Attrition Analysis
Healthcare Analytics | Python · Tableau · Segmentation · Employee Profiling

[→ View Repository](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/Employee-Attrition-Analysis) | [→ View Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/EmployeeAttrition_17799308935310/AttritionAnalysis?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

Analyzed 1,676 employee records across three clinical departments — Cardiology, Maternity, and Neurology — to identify the structural drivers behind a 12% annual attrition rate. Built a six-phase analysis covering demographics, compensation, overtime, satisfaction, and career progression, culminating in a stakeholder slide deck and interactive Tableau dashboard.

Key finding: overtime workers are 28% of the workforce but responsible for 70% of all departures — a 5.8× rate gap. The analysis surfaces a consistent high-risk profile: employees under 35, earning in the bottom salary quartile, in their first two years, and working overtime leave at 55% — nearly five times the company average. Recommendations target overtime scheduling, entry-level pay floors, and a structured first-year retention program.

### 2. Sales & Marketing Conversion Analysis
**Business Analytics / Marketing Analytics | Tableau · Excel · Customer Segmentation · Scenario Analysis**

[→ View Repository](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/Sales-Marketing-Conversion-Analysis) | [→ View Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/ProductMarketing_17794131190710/Dashboard1?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

Analyzed customer and sales data for a tourism company to identify which segments were most likely to purchase travel packages after being pitched. Built an interactive Tableau dashboard and consulting-style slide deck around conversion rate, trips sold, customer share, age groups, traveler type, income, occupation, city tier, and package type.

Key finding: the company's overall conversion rate was 19.3%, with stronger conversion among 18–29 customers, couples/pair travelers, lower-income groups, and Tier 3 cities. Scenario analysis estimated that raising 30+ customer conversion to the company average could lift total package sales by approximately 14%, while improving family/group travelers to match couples/pairs could add roughly 64 additional package sales.

### 3. Bank Marketing Conversion Driver Analysis
**Business Analytics / Marketing Analytics | SQL · Databricks · Star Schema Design · Excel**

[→ View Repository](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/bank-marketing-conversion-driver-analysis)

Modeled 41,176 telemarketing contacts from a Portuguese bank's term deposit campaign (2008–2013) into a star schema in Databricks, then used SQL to identify which customer segments, contact histories, and economic conditions predict conversion — while explicitly excluding a leakage-prone feature (call duration, only known after a call happens) from all targeting logic.

Key finding: customers who are both in a high-converting life-stage group (students, retirees, and the 18–25/66+ age brackets) and have prior contact history convert at 44.3%, nearly 4× the 11.27% baseline. Capping outreach at 3 attempts per lead was estimated to cut 18.5% of call volume while forgoing only 12.0% of conversions — an estimated $38,170 in call center savings at a cited industry cost-per-call figure.

### 4. Clinical Diabetes Risk Audit
**Healthcare Analytics | SQL · Databricks**

[→ View Repository](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/diabetes-readmission-analysis) | [→ View Deliverable](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/diabetes-readmission-analysis/blob/main/Diabetes_Readmission_Deck.pdf)

Analyzed 101,766 diabetic patient encounters across 130 U.S. hospitals to identify which patients drive Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program (HRRP) financial penalties. Isolated prior inpatient visit history as the single strongest predictor of early readmission (8.4% → 43.3% across risk tiers) and resolved an initially unexplained elevated-risk signal in young adult patients, tracing it to the same driver. Recommended a zero-infrastructure flagging rule — 2+ prior visits — that hospitals can act on with data already collected at discharge.

### 5. Quantium Retail Analytics
**Business Analytics | Python · scipy · A/B Testing**

[→ View Repository](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/quantium-retail-analytics) | [→ View Deliverable](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/quantium-retail-analytics/blob/main/Quantium_Retail_Analytics_Deliverable.pdf)

Evaluated retail trial performance across 265,000 transactions to determine whether selected trial stores generated measurable sales improvement. Matched control stores using correlation-based similarity and tested sales differences using confidence intervals.

Key insight: 2 of 3 trial stores showed statistically significant sales uplift, supporting targeted rollout decisions and showing how store-level experimentation can guide retail strategy.

[→ View Repository](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/restaurant-operations-analytics) | [→ View Deliverable](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/restaurant-operations-analytics/blob/main/Restaurant_Analytics_Deck.pdf)
