### 1. Sales & Marketing Conversion Analysis
**Business Analytics / Marketing Analytics | Tableau · Excel · Customer Segmentation · Scenario Analysis**

[→ View Repository](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/Sales-Marketing-Conversion-Analysis)| [→ View Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/ProductMarketing_17794131190710/Dashboard1?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link) | → View Slide Deck

Analyzed customer and sales data for a tourism company to identify which segments were most likely to purchase travel packages after being pitched. Built an interactive Tableau dashboard and consulting-style slide deck around conversion rate, trips sold, customer share, age groups, traveler type, income, occupation, city tier, and package type.

Key finding: the company’s overall conversion rate was 19.3%, with stronger conversion among 18–29 customers, couples/pair travelers, lower-income groups, and Tier 3 cities. Scenario analysis estimated that raising 30+ customer conversion to the company average could lift total package sales by approximately 14%, while improving family/group travelers to match couples/pairs could add roughly 64 additional package sales.

### 4. Athlete Load Monitoring & Injury Risk Prediction
**Sports Performance Analytics | Python · XGBoost · SHAP · K-Means**

[→ View Repository](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/athlete-load-injury-risk) | [→ View Deliverable](https://github.com/Hugoortega1025/athlete-load-injury-risk/blob/main/Athlete_Load_Injury_Risk_Deliverable.pdf)

Built an end-to-end injury risk pipeline using seven years of longitudinal training data from 74 elite Dutch distance runners. Engineered the Acute-to-Chronic Workload Ratio as the primary load spike signal, finding that athletes training at ACWR >= 1.5 showed a 9x higher injury rate. XGBoost achieved an AUC-ROC of 0.7483, while K-Means clustering identified three distinct training load profiles.
